How it started

A radio program, a preschool,and three schools later.

John Moran did not arrive in education from education. His career was in tradeshows, retail and wholesaling, and the turn came while he was listening to a business radio program: an Australian entrepreneur describing how he bought daycare centers. That was the idea that moved him into the service industry.

He opened Pineapple Cove Academy as a preschool and applied one principle to it, the same one he had carried through every business before: a quality product at a reasonable price. The first school enrolled about 420 children. What followed came from the same place. Build something good, then build the company that can keep it good at scale. Classical Education Management now supports three classical charter schools in Brevard County, two of which have been designated Florida High-Performing Charter Schools.

Everything behind the classroom

Finance, human resources, compliance, governance and food service are run centrally by his company, so the people in each building can spend their day on the children instead of the paperwork.

Teachers who are trained, not just hired

Orton-Gillingham based phonics, Singapore Math, classical pedagogy and classroom best practices are taught to staff directly, year after year, rather than left to chance.

New schools from the ground up

Leadership development and new school startup, from the first board conversation through to the first day of class, with the same standards carried into each new building.
